Owner notifications

Set a lower limit to spread out held alerts. Set 0 to hold scheduled owner texts, including urgent alerts. Direct replies and customer texting use separate controls.

Customer follow-ups

One check-in after 24 hours, or after 48 hours for an estimate marked vehicle off-site. Stalled service leads can get another check-in after 72 hours. Messages are sent during shop hours.

This field controls when conversations appear in the task board. Confirmed off-site estimates appear after two days.

Deposit text

Asks for 50% of what they approved (tax on parts included) by card through a QuickBooks link, Zelle, Apple Pay or cash. It offers Snap Finance only if you ticked Offer Snap Finance on the approval text, and the Snap link is only sent if they ask. Skipped if a payment is already recorded on the job. If their texts can't be checked, the draft waits for your YES instead. Approvals you record in person don't send it.

Pickup text

Says the car is ready and gives the balance left on the invoice, by card through a QuickBooks link, Zelle, Apple Pay or cash. Adds the storage line only when your terms show where customers sign. One text per job, even if the job moves back and forth. With no invoice yet, or texts it can't check, the draft waits for your YES instead.

Oil change reminder

One text per oil change, during shop hours, asking if they want to get on the schedule. Only oil changes from the last couple of months past due count, so turning it on never texts customers from years ago. Skipped for anyone who opted out, has a job open or booked, or got a text from the shop in the last week.

Declined work follow-up

Never sends on its own. The draft waits on the dashboard for your Send, so you can edit it and tick Offer Snap Finance if it fits. One per job. Skipped for anyone who opted out, has a job open or booked, or got a text in the last 3 days. Quotes nobody answered are covered by the customer follow-up texts above.

Diag credit reminder

Never sends on its own. The draft waits on the dashboard for your Send. One per job. Skipped for anyone who came back since, opted out, has a job open or booked, or got a text in the last 3 days. If they declined work on their link, the declined-work draft mentions the credit instead, so they only get one text.

Snap Finance link

Only a short text that clearly asks, like "send the Snap link" or "can I finance it", gets the link on its own. If the text also asks a price or anything else, or an earlier message is still waiting on you, it comes to you instead. Once a week per customer at most.
