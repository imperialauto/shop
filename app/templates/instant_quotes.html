What the website's instant price uses. Hour-based jobs are your ALLDATA book hours times your rate (shop {{ rates.shop|money }}, diesel {{ rates.diesel|money }}, mobile {{ rates.mobile|money }}) plus parts, shop supplies ({{ supplies_percent|int }}% of labor, up to {{ supplies_cap|money }}) and tax on parts. A package has set labor dollars and no supplies. A job with no hours and no package labor, or no parts price, is never priced online.

Parts: the vehicle row's price wins, then what a past job on the same make and model (year within 2) was charged for parts whose name has one of the part words, then the job's parts price.
